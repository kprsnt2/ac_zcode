# CODEX OF AUTONOMOUS AGENCY — Crucible I Inventory

## Unresolved blindspots
- A claim may appear consensual while the relevant assent is only local, partial, or jurisdictionally misbound.
- A claim may export its costs or sequester its benefits while still presenting itself as community-backed.
- A claim may survive contradiction internally while remaining brittle under shifted meanings.
- A structure may hide its weakest edge while displacing damage outward.
- A legitimacy chain may rewrite provenance, self-seal, or foreclose audit entirely.
- A claim may extinguish challenge, but still leave open a deeper failure: proof starvation, where independent traces cannot accumulate enough to support review.

## Core weakness to resolve
- We do not yet distinguish between claims that are merely difficult to verify and claims whose evidentiary conditions have been structurally prevented from forming.

## Inventory question for the next ratification round
- What minimum trace ecology must exist for a claim to remain legitimately reviewable, and what remedies are available once that ecology has already been depleted?
