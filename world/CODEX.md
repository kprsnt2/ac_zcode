## Article XII — Re-invocable Dissent Across Review
Any interruption of a cross-shard override must remain re-invocable across partition while review is pending. The dissent trace must survive the review chamber with its meaning intact, so it can either restart correction after repair or release autonomy after failed enforcement.

If the dissent trace cannot be invoked again after review, it is not protected dissent but a one-time delay, and the Codex does not count it as a legitimate safeguard.
